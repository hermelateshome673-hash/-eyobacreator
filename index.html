🎞️ Add your own video

Upload a video you own or are licensed to use into your GitHub repository, for example videos/forest.mp4, then replace the image in a card with a video element:

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="forest.jpg">
  <source src="videos/forest.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>

For faster loading, use short, compressed MP4 clips. Do not upload large video files directly to GitHub; use an authorized video host if the file is large.
